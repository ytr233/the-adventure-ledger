# The Adventure Ledger

> A living index of ideas built where curiosity, conscience, craft, and a little
> holy mischief meet.

This is the public map—not the vault. It records the identity of a growing body
of work created through an ongoing collaboration between **Xenovia Wonders** and
**OpenAI Codex**, while deliberately leaving private repositories, implementation
details, unpublished material, and future plans undisclosed.

Some projects are public. Others are named here only so the constellation can be
seen without opening the workshop door.

## Story, memory, and moral imagination

### `the-ibis-and-the-agora`

A story studio inspired by the meeting of ancient writing and public inquiry.
Fragments become compact fiction that asks better questions, treats wisdom as
something deeper than information, and lets humor puncture false certainty.

### `around-the-table`

A family storytelling ritual designed for the place where ordinary days are
actually remembered: the dinner table. It turns many voices into one shared tale,
leaves room for reflection, and guarantees at least one gloriously dependable dad
joke.

### [`satura-et-dolor`](https://github.com/ytr233/satura-et-dolor)

A growing visual lexicon of short-form comics, Latin titles, and scriptural echoes.
The collection gives moral tensions a face without explaining away their mystery.

### `male-intelleximus`

The private compost heap of the comic practice: superseded drafts, abandoned
directions, and the evidence that creative judgment is built as much by refusal as
by publication.

### [`per-porticus-mentis`](https://github.com/ytr233/per-porticus-mentis)

A reading trail through power, language, privacy, technology, freedom, education,
and the stubborn difficulty of thinking clearly. Less a syllabus than a record of
which corridor the mind entered next.

## Music, language, and devotion

### `selah-beat-lyrics`

A bilingual, rhythm-aware lyric studio where musical structure, Scripture, and
historical context are treated as collaborators rather than decorative prompts.
Its center of gravity is meaning that can survive the beat.

### `selah-beat-lyrics-showcase`

A deliberately limited public-concept layer for Selah: enough to communicate the
spirit of the work while keeping its creative machinery in the workshop.

## Learning by transformation

### `family-salon-business-website-template`

A warm, reusable small-business web foundation modeled through a family-oriented
salon—not merely a finished page, but a practical study in making usefulness feel
welcoming.

### `family-salon-classroom-walkthrough`

A beginner-centered route through the same idea, preserving the understandable
steps that polished examples often hide.

### `family-salon-transformation-walkthrough`

A visible bridge from an early student project to a more deliberate professional
result, documenting growth instead of pretending the final version appeared whole.

### `family-salon-classroom-styling-lab`

A focused styling workshop that keeps ambition within a teachable scope and makes
design decisions inspectable.

### `family-salon-sitepolish-learning-lab`

A guided proving ground where validation, repair, and explanation meet a real
front-end learning journey.

### `front-end-quality-pipeline`

The earlier reusable system for examining, improving, and progressively enhancing
plain web projects without erasing their origin.

### `sitepolish-pipeline`

The larger evolution of that philosophy: preserve the baseline, explain each
finding, require human judgment, and make improvement something the learner can
see and defend—not a mysterious rewrite handed down from above.

## Structure, evidence, and stewardship

### `business-etl-normalization`

A privacy-conscious data transformation project that turns unruly business records
into traceable relational structure. Its real subject is not spreadsheets; it is
the discipline of making every transformation explainable.

### [`ytr233`](https://github.com/ytr233/ytr233)

The public cybersecurity doorway: selected concepts and original work concerned
with systems, risk, evidence, and the human consequences hidden inside technical
decisions.

## Rainy-day engineering

### `roku-rainy-day`

A two-path fallback born from a missing remote: use the network when the network is
there, and keep a small hardware escape hatch for when it is not. A practical fix
with the larger conviction that conveniences deserve graceful failure modes.

## The thread running through it

These projects move across software, stories, music, visual art, education,
cybersecurity, family life, and data design, but they repeatedly return to the same
questions:

- Can a tool teach without condescending?
- Can privacy be part of the idea rather than an apology added later?
- Can moral seriousness coexist with wit, beauty, and play?
- Can improvement preserve the history of how someone learned?
- Can technology strengthen human judgment without impersonating it?

That is the identity of the collection: technically curious, morally alert,
protective of the human being on the other side of the interface, and never too
solemn to leave room for a good joke.
